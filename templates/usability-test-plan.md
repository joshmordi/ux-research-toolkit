# Usability Test Plan

## 1. Purpose
What decision will this test inform? State the goal in one or two sentences.

## 2. Research questions
List the specific questions the test must answer. Keep it to three to five.

## 3. Method
- Moderated or unmoderated
- Remote or in person
- Think-aloud protocol: yes / no

## 4. Participants
- Number: 5 to 8 per user group (enough to surface major issues)
- Profile: who they are and why they qualify
- Recruitment: how participants will be found and screened
- Incentive: what participants receive

## 5. Tasks
List each task with its scenario (see `task-scenarios.md`). For each task, note:
- The starting point
- What counts as success
- What will be measured (success, time, errors, satisfaction)

## 6. Metrics
- Task success rate
- Time on task
- Error count and type
- SUS score (after the session)
- Qualitative observations

## 7. Materials
- Consent form
- Task scenarios (printed or on screen, one at a time)
- SUS questionnaire
- Note-taking sheet

## 8. Schedule
| Time | Activity |
| ---- | -------- |
| 0:00 | Welcome and consent |
| 0:05 | Background questions |
| 0:10 | Tasks |
| 0:40 | SUS questionnaire and debrief |
| 0:50 | Wrap up |

## 9. Reporting
Findings go into `findings-report.md`, with severity ratings and clips or quotes as evidence.
