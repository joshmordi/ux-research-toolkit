# Usability Findings Report

## Summary
Three to five sentences: what was tested, with whom, and the headline result. A busy reader should get the point from this section alone.

## Method
- Participants: number and profile
- Tasks: how many, and what kind
- Metrics: success rate, time on task, SUS score, observations

## Findings
One section per issue, ordered by severity. Each finding needs evidence: a quote, an observation, or a metric. No evidence, no finding.

### Finding 1: [short title]
- **Severity:** critical / major / minor / cosmetic
- **What happened:** describe what participants did
- **Evidence:** "quote or observation"
- **Recommendation:** what to change

## Severity scale
- **Critical:** blocks the task; users cannot complete it
- **Major:** causes significant delay or frustration
- **Minor:** causes brief confusion but users recover
- **Cosmetic:** polish; fix if time allows

## Appendix
Task list, participant profiles, full SUS scores, session notes.
